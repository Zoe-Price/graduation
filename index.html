Tuesday May 26
1:15 – 2:00 PM · Old Yard, Harvard Yard
Baccalaureate Procession
The Class of 2026 processes around Old Yard into Tercentenary Theatre. Procession starts at the corner of Holworthy and Thayer, passing University Hall before entering between University Hall and Weld. Seniors should arrive by 1:15 PM. Guests are welcome to observe.
No ticket required Regalia required
2:00 – 3:30 PM · Tercentenary Theatre, Harvard Yard
Baccalaureate Ceremony
President Garber shares remarks with the Class of 2026 and students offer readings from their religious, spiritual, and ethical traditions. Seniors sit in assigned sections near the stage; guests take remaining sections. After the program, guests should remain seated while seniors process to Widener Library Steps.
No ticket required Regalia required
3:30 – 4:00 PM · Widener Library Steps
Class of 2026 Photograph
Graduating seniors process from Tercentenary Theatre to the steps of Widener Library for the official class photograph. Rain date: Wednesday May 27, immediately following Class Day Exercises. Photos can be ordered at the Panfoto website (link forthcoming).
No ticket required Seniors only Regalia required
4:00 – 5:00 PM · Old Yard, Harvard Yard
Dessert Reception (tentative)
A celebratory dessert reception in Old Yard for graduating seniors and their guests, following the Baccalaureate Ceremony and class photograph.
No ticket required Seniors + guests
8:00 – 10:00 PM · Tercentenary Theatre, Harvard Yard
Harvard University Band & Harvard Choruses Concert
An annual evening concert featuring Harvard's student performers. Open to all members of the Harvard community — a beloved tradition to close the first evening of Commencement Week.
No ticket required
Wednesday May 27
Noon – 2:00 PM · Old Yard, Harvard Yard
Class Day Picnic
A casual bagged lunch before Class Day Exercises. Harvard Yard closes at 11:00 AM; entry by ticket scan at four gates: Widener, Johnston, Meyer/Thayer, and Sever. Staff exchange picnic tickets for a bagged lunch inside the Yard.
Tickets required
2:00 – 4:00 PM · Tercentenary Theatre, Harvard Yard
Class Day Exercises
The Class of 2026's own celebration — featuring the Class Day speaker, Harvard Oration, Ivy Oration, Ames Awards, Class Ode, and remarks from Dean David Deming. No assigned seating; seniors sit with family. No regalia — business casual. Streamed at harvard.edu/live and YouTube.
Tickets required Streamed online
4:30 – 6:30 PM · McKinlock Courtyard, Leverett House
Leverett Class Day Family Reception (DHall if rain)
Leverett House hosts graduating seniors and their families in McKinlock Courtyard after Class Day. Food, drinks, and a chance to introduce families to the Faculty Deans and to each other. Leverett starts earlier than most houses so families can come straight from the Yard, and so student-athletes can attend before their 5 PM dinner.
No tickets required Seniors + families
Thursday May 28 — Commencement Day
Early morning (time TBD) · Leverett House
Leverett Senior Breakfast
Leverett House hosts a senior breakfast on Commencement morning before seniors make their way up to the Yard for the procession. More details from Leverett staff closer to the date.
No ticket required Leverett seniors
8:00 – 8:15 AM · Harvard Yard, near Holden Chapel
Senior Valediction
A brief seniors-only gathering just before the Commencement procession lineup. Seniors march into the Yard with their Houses. Families and guests should already be finding their seats in Tercentenary Theatre. Wear regalia.
Graduating seniors only Regalia required
9:30 AM – 12:15 PM · Tercentenary Theatre, Harvard Yard
375th Harvard University Commencement Exercises
The University-wide ceremony celebrating all Harvard graduates. Features student remarks, conferral of degrees, honorary degrees, and the Commencement Speaker. Seniors process in by House. Names are not called and diplomas are not presented here — that happens at the Leverett ceremony. Streamed at harvard.edu/live. Yard gates open at 6:45 AM.
Tickets required for guests Regalia required (seniors) Streamed online
Immediately after Commencement (~12:15 PM) · Leverett House
Leverett House Ceremony & Luncheon
Leverett holds its ceremony immediately after the Yard exercises and before lunch — please return to the house quickly so the ceremony can begin. Graduates' names are called and diplomas are presented here.

If outdoors — Towers Courtyard: Unlimited guests welcome to watch the ceremony. Lunch tickets required to eat.

If indoors — Malkin Athletic Center (MAC): Each graduate may bring 4 guests inside. Additional guests may watch a livestream from common spaces or your room, then join for lunch immediately after.
Lunch requires ticket Ceremony: free to watch Regalia required
4:00 PM · Carpenter Center for the Visual Arts
AFVS Class of 2026 Commencement Party
The Art, Film & Visual Studies department's celebration for the graduating Class of 2026. No tickets or registration needed — just show up. A chance to celebrate with fellow AFVS students, faculty, and friends after the main ceremonies.
Nothing required
